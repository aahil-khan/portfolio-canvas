Essays don't need an artifact, so when one is pinned the featured slot shows its opening lines instead of a window. The home keeps the same grid, the same headings and the same order of blocks, which is what the check compares between the two builds.

The featured slot is the one place on the home that changes with the data. Everything around it is fixed: the intro, Latest, the tours and the sections. If a pinned item of a new kind forced the grid to move, every future pin would be a design job, and that is exactly the kind of overfitting this fixture is here to catch before it ships.

So the check builds the home twice, once with the real content and once with these fixtures, and compares the left edge and width of every block. A fourth section adds rows, and this essay replaces the window with a quote, but the shape of the page stays put.

## A heading that belongs to the fixture essay

The rest of this essay is short. It exists to give the template a long first paragraph to measure and a heading no other page uses. Real essays in Task 3 run to several hundred words and carry one pull-quote each, taken word for word from their own text.
