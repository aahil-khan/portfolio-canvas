The Lab, on the canvas

Two ways for the Lab to live inside the canvas rather than beside it. Both use the same desk, the same 19 pieces and the same star layout, and differ only in how you get to the sky. Open each one and decide by eye.

Direction 1

Sky beyond the desk

Pan or zoom out past the desk and the cream floor fades into a jet starfield, where the constellations live. The deep-space card becomes the door, the Lab dock tile flies you there, and /lab deep-links to it. The index is an ordinary canvas window.

Direction 2

Night mode

The Lab dock tile turns the whole canvas to night. The desk's cards make way, the pieces come out as stars and constellations, and one control brings the desk back.

Star layouts, from data alone

shared/layout.js places every star from shelf, constellation, date and links, with no coordinates anywhere in the content. Add a piece and watch: hollow rings are where the stars were before, and none of them moves. Any shape can be centred on a world point at a set size (centre, extent), say around the deep-space card, or across night mode's screen.
