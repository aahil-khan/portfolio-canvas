The first time a note uses one of these words, it has a dotted underline. Hover it or tap it for the line below.

## eval

A repeatable test of an AI system, scored the same way every time. Run after every change, it shows what broke.

## pass@k

The chance that at least one of k tries at a task succeeds. It asks whether the agent can do the task at all.

## pass^k

The chance that all k tries at a task succeed. It asks whether the agent does it every time, which is what a customer needs.

## LLM-as-judge

A language model grading another model's answers against a rubric. It lets judgement calls be scored at scale.

## MCC

One number, −1 to 1, for how well a yes-or-no judge matches the truth. The Matthews correlation coefficient stays honest when one answer is much rarer than the other.

## OTel

OpenTelemetry: an open standard for traces, metrics and logs. It lets every step an agent takes be recorded and inspected.

## ZOPA

Zone of possible agreement: the prices both sides would accept. If it is empty, the right outcome is no sale.

## PW

A project stage: one piece of a project, built, measured and written up. Its ID is PW and a number.

## T

A study topic: one thing to learn, with hours, a log and a proof. Its ID is T and two digits.

## W

A guided tour: a reading path through several notes, in order. Its ID is W and a number.
