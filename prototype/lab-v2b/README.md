# Lab v2, version B: the reading pages

Static mockup of the Lab (plan `lab-v2-b-a`, tasks 1 and 2, plus Aahil's amendments of 27 Nov), in two looks. Task 1 built the home and
chapter 05 (revised 27 Nov: an evergreen home, Wikipedia-style inline links). Task 2 builds every
other page of the Lab: section pages, `/lab/index`, the study, notes T01 and T02, the Shopkeeper hub
and all eight chapters, the three builds, both essays, a page per tag, a glossary, and the experiment
template (proved through the fixture).
Fresh code. Nothing here comes from `prototype/lab/`, and there are no npm dependencies.

```bash
node --experimental-strip-types import.mjs   # regenerate content/lab-data.js from the real sources
node bundle.mjs                              # one self-contained file -> ~/projects/scratch/lab-mockups/lab-v2b.html
node bundle.mjs --copy                       # rewrite COPY.md from content/copy.js
cx heavy -- node check.mjs                   # the checklist; ends with LAB-V2B OK
```

`index.html` also opens straight from disk while you work.

Routes are generated from the data, never listed by hand (`Lab.routes()`): `#/`, `#/<section>` for
every section with a public entry, `#/index`, `#/glossary`, `#/tags/<tag>`, and every public entry at
`#/<section>/<entry>` or `#/<section>/<parent>/<child>`. Only the rest of the site (`/`, the desk, the
colophon, For AI agents) still goes to the one-line holding page; those come in task 3.

## Body blocks

A body (`content/bodies-*.js`) is a list of blocks: `{ h2 }`, `{ p }`, `{ list, ordered }`,
`{ quote, cite }`, `{ code }`, `{ side, sideHead }` (a sidenote in the margin column),
`{ img: { src, w, h, alt, caption } }`, and `{ figure: '<instrument>', of: '<data key>' }` (`compare`
and `chart` read `Lab.compares` / `Lab.charts`). Beside the blocks: `refs` (numbered by first use),
`pull` (a verbatim sentence of the body; the check refuses one that isn't), `leadIntro` (the bold line
over an interactive lead), and for builds and experiments `ending` + `endingKind` (`do`, `use` or
`measure`), rendered as "What I'd do now", dated *as of* the entry's last update so it never reads stale.
Read time is computed from the words (220 a minute).

## Section pages are evergreen too

Each section page is the only kind of page with a hero, and the hero is data: the section's `hero`
entry (Studies picks the agent-reliability study, so its board leads), or, with no pick, the three
newest top-level entries as a strip. Under the thesis, **New here?** says what the section holds (a
count by kind, from the data) and where to begin by kind: the oldest entry of the highest-ranked kind
the section holds (study, series, build, experiment, essay). The oldest never changes when something
is added, so the line stays true. The check (rule H3) holds the furniture to the home's time-word list.

## Related, previews and the end of every entry

Every entry ends with **Links to this page** (entries that link here or name it in data; "No other
page links here." when none do) and **Related**: entries this one links to, then "also related" by
shared tags, and if both are empty, "also in" its own section, so Related is never empty. Every link
to an entry, section or tag opens that whole page in a window; terms, references, links out and the
board's squares open a card (tap on a phone; hover or the arrow keys on desktop, with the grid as one
tab stop).

The phone sheet is one surface now (review of #35): it draws only its top edge, and its title row
sits on the same ground as its text. The footer's build hash is written by `bundle.mjs` from
`git rev-parse --short HEAD` at bundle time (rule F checks it).

## The home is evergreen

It names no entry, series or project in its own furniture. From the top: the intro (his name says
itself), **New here?** (what the Lab is, how to read it, where to start: by section), **Sections**,
**Latest** (the six newest entries, any kind, absolute handwritten dates), then "All N entries →" and
the footer. No Now line, nothing pinned, no progress, no relative dates.

**Sections** (amended 27 Nov: "there is no clear separation bw sections, and they should list like the
first 5 entries of each"): each section is its own framed band, the window frame of the look, with its
heading, thesis and count on the left and its first five entries by date on the right (handwritten
date, title, kind), then "All N in <section> →". On a phone the two halves stack. The check (H1) holds
every band to `min(5, n)` rows in date order, its "All N in" link, a frame, and a gap from the next;
H2 still forbids time words (so the furniture never says "newest") and anything that counts progress.

## His name

His underlined name has its own mark, a double underline, never a term's dotted one. Hover it (tap on
a phone, or focus it) and a small tooltip says just **AH-hil**, and the name is spoken once: no title,
no button, no link. Resting on the name doesn't repeat it; leaving and coming back does; each tap on a
phone is one more. It speaks with the device's voice (`speechSynthesis`, an `en-IN` voice when there is
one, which says "Aahil" right; otherwise the spelling of the sound, "Ah-hil").

- **The one exception to muted on arrival.** Interface sounds being off (the default) doesn't stop it.
  Only an explicit **Mute** (the speaker button beside Master in the Sound menu, `Lab.audio.muted`)
  silences it.
- **The gesture lock.** Browsers refuse speech, and any audio, until the page has had a user gesture
  (Chrome's autoplay policy; `navigator.userActivation.hasBeenActive` says whether one has happened).
  So a hover before the visitor has clicked anything shows the tooltip silently and queues the name;
  the first click or key press while the tooltip shows says it. After that, every hover speaks. A
  queued name is dropped when the pointer leaves, so a later click elsewhere never says it out of
  nowhere. Keyboard focus shows the tooltip; Enter or Space says it, so a screen reader announcing the
  tooltip isn't talked over.

## Windows: a link to a Lab page opens the whole page

Amended 27 Nov: "it shouldnt show a preview only with open it button, it should be fully viewable and
scrollable within the hover window, only external links get the previews".

- A link to an entry, a section or a tag opens a window holding that **whole page**: lead (at card
  size), body, figures, references and the ends. It is read and scrolled inside the window
  (`overscroll-behavior: contain`; the wheel over its title bar scrolls its body too), so the page
  behind never moves. The page's own title, linked, is the way through. No "Open it", no excerpt.
- **Stacking.** A link inside a window opens a further window, cascaded to the right of it, up to
  three. The stack stays while the pointer is anywhere on it or on the link that opened it, and the
  hover-opened windows close 220ms after the pointer leaves for the page. Esc closes the top window
  only; a click outside the top window closes it (a click inside a lower one closes what's above).
  A click on a link itself still navigates.
- **Ids.** Every id inside a window, and every reference to one (`aria-*`, `for`, `data-jump`, `#`
  hrefs), is prefixed (`w1-`, `w2-` …), so a citation in a window jumps within that window.
- **Phone.** A tap opens the whole page as a near-full-height sheet (`max-height: 92vh`), one surface
  with only its top edge drawn, scrolled inside, with a 44px Close. A link inside it opens the next
  sheet; a second tap on the same words follows the link.
- **Cards stay cards.** Terms keep their definition cards, references their source cards, board
  squares their topic cards. **Links out** (the References list, a build's "Source on GitHub" and
  "Open it live", a term card's source) get a small card: title, site, one line, built from every
  entry's `refs` and the builds' `repo` and `live`, falling back to the hostname.

The check (W) opens a window from chapter 05, compares its paragraphs with the page's, wheels inside
it and asserts the page didn't scroll, stacks three, presses Esc once, and hovers a link out.

## Inline links, the Wikipedia model

Markup in any copy or body text:

| Write | Renders | Preview |
|---|---|---|
| `[[id\|words]]` | an entry link, solid underline | the whole entry, in a window |
| `[[t:id\|words]]` | a term, dotted underline | the term's name and one-line definition |
| `[[tag:id\|words]]` | a tag, dotted underline | the tag's page, in a window |
| `[^key]` | a numbered superscript citation | the source's title, site and one line |

- **Desktop** (a fine pointer that hovers): a mini canvas window by the words, after a 300ms hover
  intent, kept open while the pointer is on it, closed 220ms after it leaves. Focus opens it too.
- **Touch:** the first tap opens a sheet at the bottom with a Close button, scrolled so the tapped
  words stay above it; the sheet's own link goes further, and a second tap on the same words follows
  the link. An outside tap or Esc closes it.
- Underlines are `text-decoration` in `--ink-muted` with an offset, never a colour wash; the check
  holds every inline underline to 3:1 against its ground in each look (rule U).
- No interface sound on hover. The `peek` cue plays only when a window opens from a tap, click or focus.
  His name is the one thing a hover says aloud (above).

Two designs were weighed for phones: a popover anchored under the tapped words (as on desktop), and
a bottom sheet. At 390px a 420px-wide card can't sit beside the words, so the popover would cover the
next lines of the paragraph being read. The sheet leaves the tapped words in view (it scrolls them
clear when needed) and always carries a 44px Close, so the sheet is what's built.

## The Sound menu: the canvas's own

Amended 27 Nov: "sound section also looks a bit off, also its too big, i love how canvas handles it".
The side panel and its page reflow are gone. The bar's small Sound pill opens a compact drop-down that
is the canvas's menu (`components/desktop/sound-menu.tsx`, `app/desktop.css`) row for row and size for
size: **Interface sounds** (switch and hint), **Ambience** (switch and "playing · <station>"), the six
stations with **shuffle** and the now-playing equaliser, the **mixer** (Master, with a chevron that
reveals Music, Noise and Interface), and a one-line credit. Its frame is the look's window frame.

The Lab's two extras couldn't fit beside the stations at the canvas's footprint (six stations, five
noises and Tracks would be about 640px tall), so a small segmented row, **Radio · Noise · Tracks**, sits
where the canvas prints "Station", and the three lists share one slot, so switching never resizes the
menu. Noise lists the five textures and "Play under the music"; Tracks says why it's empty (task 3).
The one explicit **Mute** is the speaker button beside Master.

It closes on Esc and on a press outside, as on the canvas; on a phone it also closes on a pick and on a
page scroll, so nothing of it stays over the text. It stores under the canvas's keys (`canvas.sound`,
`canvas.station`, `canvas.ambience`, `canvas.masterVolume`, `canvas.ambienceVolume`,
`canvas.uiVolume`), so choices carry between the desk and the Lab. Rule S holds it to the canvas's
measured 292 × 417 (±10%) at both sizes and asserts that opening it reflows nothing.

## Premium keeps the canvas's window language

Amended 27 Nov: "dont ditch canvas' design language entirely even in premium mode, it should still feel
like part of the same site". Premium is the same site in a richer palette. Every box, window, card,
figure and the Sound menu keeps the 2px `--border` frame and a zero-blur offset shadow, windows keep
their title bars (a faint accent tint over the raised tone), and the radii, type and yellow AK mark
are the site's. What Premium changes is colour, tuned per palette:

| | Night | Day |
|---|---|---|
| `--border` (the frame) | `#4A453B`, a warm umber | `#262520`, a step softer than the ink |
| `--shadow-ink` (the offset) | `#2B2822`, a step above the ground, never black on black | `#B9B4A6`, warm stone |
| `--hair` (rules between rows) | `rgba(238,236,229,.08)` | `rgba(20,20,19,.10)` |

Rule K checks every visible box, panel, window, card and the menu, in Day and Night, for a frame and a
hard offset shadow, and fails any blurred shadow.
